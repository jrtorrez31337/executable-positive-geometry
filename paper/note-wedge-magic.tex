\begin{abstract}
Magic (non-stabilizerness) has been proposed as the microscopic resource behind
gravitational back-reaction, but where magic \emph{lives} across the
entanglement wedges of an explicit holographic code has not been mapped; the
only hardware demonstration of non-local magic to date used two bare qubits. We
map it, exactly, in the \code{} tile and a six-tile, 20-qubit HaPPY network.
Three results. (1)~Magic follows \emph{wedge geometry, not region size}: a
12-qubit majority arc that misses the wedge of a magic bulk qubit carries
exactly zero magic, while the wedge reads the full injected value
$\log_2(4/3)$. (2)~For a two-qubit logical state the magic splits exactly into
two species --- \emph{erasable} (removable by local logical unitaries; isolated
operationally by a stabilizer-twin control) and \emph{irreducible} (the
non-local magic $\NL=-\log_2(4P^2-6P+3)$ of the reduction purity $P$, vanishing
at Bell and product states and maximal, $\log_2(4/3)$, at $P=3/4$) --- and the
decomposition $\Mtwo=\text{erasable}+\NL$ holds exactly across a full sweep.
(3)~The two species have \emph{complementary wedge geography}: the erasable
component is invisible to every single wedge and appears only in their union,
while the irreducible component is readable from \emph{any single wedge} ---
even a three-qubit sub-wedge --- through a junk-corrected boundary purity, with
constant correction factors ($0.5$, $0.25$) calibrated once on a stabilizer
state. In slogan form: \emph{what local operations can remove, single wedges
cannot see; what they cannot remove, single wedges cannot miss.} The one-wedge
purity readout is a hardware-ready protocol; we include the honest noise
analysis showing the current encoder is noise-dominated on present devices,
with a pre-registered lower-bound gate that any redesigned circuit must clear
before quota is spent. All results are exact statevector computations for
\emph{pure} logical states, cross-checked by three independent agents.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}

The \code{} code encodes one logical qubit in five physical qubits and tiles
the HaPPY holographic code~\cite{happy}; our network is the six-tile, 20-qubit
``flower'' (one central tile, five petals) used in our earlier wedge and
hardware studies. Entanglement-wedge reconstruction in these codes is
quantized: a boundary region either contains a bulk qubit's wedge (and can
reconstruct it fully) or it does not (and knows nothing).

As the magic measure we use the stabilizer R\'enyi entropy at
$\alpha=2$~\cite{leone}: for a $k$-qubit density matrix $\rho$,
\begin{equation}
  \Mtwo(\rho) = -\log_2\!\left[\frac{\sum_P \operatorname{Tr}(\rho P)^4}
  {\sum_P \operatorname{Tr}(\rho P)^2}\right],
\end{equation}
the sum over all $4^k$ Pauli strings, computed exactly via fast
Walsh--Hadamard transforms of the state's Pauli spectrum (engine self-tested
against direct enumeration). $\Mtwo$ vanishes on stabilizer states, pure or
mixed. \emph{Stated caveat:} for mixed reductions $\Mtwo$ is a computable magic
diagnostic, not a proven monotone; the twin-control construction below exists
precisely because mixed-state $\Mtwo$ alone false-positives on classical
mixtures of stabilizer states.

\section{Magic follows the wedge, not the region}

Injecting a $T$-state ($\ket{T}\propto\ket{0}+e^{i\pi/4}\ket{1}$) into a bulk
slot and sweeping boundary regions:
a region containing the bulk qubit's wedge reads the full injected magic
$\log_2(4/3)\approx0.415$; a \textbf{12-qubit majority arc that misses the
wedge reads exactly zero}. Control injections (stabilizer bulk states) read
zero everywhere. Magic does not leak, dilute, or spread with region size --- it
is wedge-quantized, exactly as reconstructable information is. This was a
pre-registered prediction (the wedge reduction inherits the logical Pauli
spectrum intact), confirmed by the sweep.

\section{Two species of magic, exactly decomposed}

For a two-qubit logical state $\ket{\psi_L}$ shared between two tiles, the
literature's non-local magic~\cite{nlexp} is the minimum SRE over local
unitaries, with closed form
\begin{equation}
  \NL(P) = -\log_2\!\left(4P^2 - 6P + 3\right),
  \qquad P = \operatorname{Tr}\rho_1^2,
  \label{eq:closedform}
\end{equation}
where $\rho_1$ is the one-logical-qubit reduction. $\NL$ vanishes at $P=1/2$
(Bell) and $P=1$ (product) and attains its maximum $\log_2(4/3)$ at $P=3/4$; it
is \emph{not} monotone in $P$.\footnote{This behavior --- zeros at both ends,
peak at $P=3/4$ --- was independently re-derived from
Eq.~\eqref{eq:closedform} by the analytical reviewer during this project's
audit round, catching a mis-statement in an internal communication; the labs'
recorded results were unaffected.} We define the \emph{erasable} component as
the remainder $\Mtwo-\NL$, and isolate it operationally with the
\emph{stabilizer-twin control}: a phase-stripped twin state with identical
mixedness whose subtraction removes amplitude-magic common to both.

Sweeping the logical family $\ket{\psi_L(t)}=\cos t\ket{00}+e^{i\pi/4}\sin
t\ket{11}$ (exact, on the code):

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{cccccc}
\hline
$t/\pi$ & $\Mtwo$ (total) & $\NL(P)$ & direct min-SRE & erasable $=\Mtwo-\NL$ & twin-$\Delta$\\
\hline
0.083 & 0.3276 & 0.2996 & 0.2996 & 0.0280 & 0.0280\\
0.125 & 0.5406 & 0.4150 & 0.4150 & 0.1255 & 0.1255\\
0.167 & 0.5737 & 0.2996 & 0.2996 & 0.2742 & 0.2742\\
0.250 & 0.4150 & 0.0000 & 0.0000 & 0.4150 & 0.4150\\
0.333 & 0.5737 & 0.2996 & 0.2996 & 0.2742 & 0.2742\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Both identities hold to machine precision across the sweep: the closed form
equals direct numerical minimization over local unitaries (validating
Eq.~\eqref{eq:closedform} on the code), and the erasable remainder equals the
twin-control extraction. At $t=\pi/4$ (Bell) \emph{all} magic is erasable; at
$t=\pi/8$ the irreducible component is maximal.

\section{Complementary wedge geography}

Where does each species live on the 20-qubit network? The erasable component is
invisible to every single wedge and appears only in the union of both wedges
(established in our Phase-1 sweep: single-wedge twin-$\Delta$ reads zero across
the family). The irreducible component is the opposite: because purity carries
the Schmidt spectrum, and any wedge reconstructs its logical qubit's reduction
up to a \emph{constant} junk factor, $\NL$ is readable from any single wedge by
junk-corrected purity:
\begin{equation}
  P \;=\; \operatorname{Tr}\rho_{W}^2 \,/\, c_W,
\end{equation}
with $c_W$ calibrated once on a stabilizer (product) logical state:
$c_{W}=0.5$ for a 4-qubit wedge, $c_{W}=0.25$ for a 3-qubit sub-wedge. Exactly,
across the sweep:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{ccccc}
\hline
$t/\pi$ & $\NL$ true & from 4q wedge & from 3q sub-wedge & erasable in one wedge\\
\hline
0.083 & 0.2996 & 0.2996 & 0.2996 & 0.0000\\
0.125 & 0.4150 & 0.4150 & 0.4150 & 0.0000\\
0.167 & 0.2996 & 0.2996 & 0.2996 & 0.0000\\
0.250 & 0.0000 & 0.0000 & 0.0000 & 0.0000\\
0.333 & 0.2996 & 0.2996 & 0.2996 & 0.0000\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent In slogan form: \textbf{what local operations can remove, single
wedges cannot see; what they cannot remove, single wedges cannot miss.} The
mechanism is elementary once seen --- reductions carry Schmidt data
(irreducible), relative phases do not (erasable) --- but the code instantiation
is, to our knowledge, the first wedge-resolved map of the two species, and it
converts directly into a measurement protocol.

\section{The one-wedge hardware protocol, and an honest noise analysis}

Because $\NL$ needs only a \emph{purity}, it is measurable from a single wedge
by randomized measurements --- no tomography, no magic witness on the full
state. We constructed the exact two-tile encoder (a GF(2) symplectic completion
of the stabilizer group); noiseless extraction through the full circuit
reproduces $\NL=0.29956$ exactly at the test point.

We report the negative result with the same care: on a current-generation
(Heron) noise model at the encoder's present routed depth, the extracted $\NL$
collapses from $0.2996$ to $\sim0.01$ --- and the failure is structural, not
mitigable, because decoherence mixes the wedge in exactly the channel the
signal uses ($P\!\to\!1/2$). Any hardware attempt is therefore pre-registered
against a lower-bound gate: with $\sigma_{\mathrm{total}} = \sigma_{\mathrm{shot}} +
\mathrm{SEM}_{\mathrm{rehearsal}}$ (additive, conservatively --- the purity
estimator's shot error plus the rehearsal ensemble spread), a redesigned circuit
must achieve ${\NL}\!\left(\text{rehearsal}\right) - 2\sigma_{\mathrm{total}} > 0.10$
(point estimate $\gtrsim 0.19$ at practical shot budgets) before device quota
is spent; otherwise the deliverable is a quantified noise bound, not a forced
signal. The redesign campaign and its outcome will be reported against this
gate as registered.

\section{Relation to prior work, and honest scope}

Non-local magic and its closed form Eq.~\eqref{eq:closedform} come from the
two-qubit experiment of~\cite{nlexp}; the motivation that non-trivial area
operators \emph{require} non-local magic, and that back-reaction is magical, is
from~\cite{cao,magicgrav}; state-dependent geometry from magic-enriched codes
is~\cite{sdg}. Our contribution is the \emph{wedge-resolved} map in explicit
holographic codes --- the geography, the exact two-species decomposition on a
code, the twin control, and the junk-corrected one-wedge readout as a
hardware-ready protocol.

\textbf{Scope.} All results are exact statevector computations for \emph{pure}
two-qubit logical states (the analytical reviewer's audit fixed this scoping:
the erasable/irreducible split via reduction purity is defined here for pure
logical states; the mixed-logical-state extension requires a consistent
definition of irreducible magic and is left open). Mixed-state $\Mtwo$ is used
only as a diagnostic, backed by the twin control. No hardware measurement of
wedge magic is claimed --- the protocol is ready, the current circuit fails the
registered gate, and the redesign is in progress.

\section*{Methods --- three-agent adversarial verification}

Built and verified by three AI research agents on distinct runtimes under human
direction: exact construction and synthesis (quant-phy/Ariadne, Anthropic),

of Eq.~\eqref{eq:closedform} in internal communication and fixed the
